# Privacy Policy — Toggle Silent Mode

**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

Toggle Silent Mode ("the app") is an Android app that switches your phone's ringer between Normal, Vibrate and Silent. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Information we collect
**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or device information.

- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send any data off your device.

## Permissions the app uses
| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Do Not Disturb access (`ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY`) | Required by Android to change the ringer mode, including switching to Silent. The app only reads and changes the ringer mode; it does not read your notifications or your Do Not Disturb rules. |
| Vibrate (`VIBRATE`) | Plays a short vibration pattern so you can feel which mode was selected. |

All of this happens locally on your device.

## Children's privacy
The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
